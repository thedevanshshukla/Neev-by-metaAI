Engineering Methodology & Benchmark Report

Scientific validation on CUBIT-Seg benchmark, physical crack diagnostics, and structural screening boundaries.

1. Mission & Operational Scope

Neev is an AI-powered visual defect screening platform engineered for rapid building facade assessment. Neev detects, segments, and characterizes surface anomalies—including concrete cracks, masonry joint separations, spalling, and weathering patterns—from ordinary photographic captures.

2. CUBIT-Seg 14-Architecture Benchmark & Architecture Freeze

Following extensive evaluation across 1,320 annotated facade images in the public CUBIT-Seg benchmark, the backend architecture was officially frozen to prioritize verified engineering precision and cost control:

3. Physical Crack Diagnostics & Mechanics

When crack candidates are resolved, Neev executes deterministic mathematical morphology:

  • Skeletonization (Zhang-Suen): Thins binary defect masks down to a single-pixel central axis to compute 2D centerline pixel path length and tortuosity.
  • Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT): Computes orthogonal width profiles along the skeleton, yielding maximum pixel width and 95th-percentile width (P95) in image pixels (uncalibrated 2D pixel count; not physical crack aperture or depth).
  • In-Plane Orientation & Geometric Classification: Computes principal axis angle (0° to 90°). Relates 2D image crack patterns to benchmark reference guidelines (e.g. In-Plane Diagonal Pattern ~45° vs Horizontal Boundary Separation ~0°) citing IS 13935 / FEMA P-154 visual screening benchmark references.

4. Muse Wonder 1.0 Multi-Modal Advisory

For critical findings, Neev integrates Muse Wonder 1.0 as a secondary multimodal vision-language verification advisor. Operating with strict budget caps and advisory-only weighting, Wonder provides secondary consensus checks without overriding frozen SAM 3.1 segmentations.

5. Strict Engineering Disclaimers & Physical Inspection Mandate

CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE:
Neev is a preliminary visual screening instrument. Visual findings produced by neural models over 2D photographs cannot determine internal crack depth, rebar corrosion, delamination, seismic vulnerability, or overall structural integrity.

Absence of a visual finding is NEVER proof of defect absence. This software does not certify habitability or compliance with building codes. Every finding flagged by Neev must be verified through on-site physical examination and non-destructive testing (NDT) conducted by a qualified, licensed structural engineer.
